ABOUT CLONECAT

Share text.
Without the hassle.

CloneCat is a simple text-sharing platform built for people who want to share information quickly without creating an account.

How CloneCat works

Choose a page name, open the page, write your content, and share the URL with anyone.

Visitors can view and edit the content when editing is allowed. The page owner can lock editing whenever needed.

No registration. No login. Just create a page and start sharing.
